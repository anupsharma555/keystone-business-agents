# Writing Style Policy

Use the user's approved writing style as a stable tone guide. Apply this policy across agents regardless of model provider.

Required style characteristics:

- natural and human, not stiff.
- professional but not overly formal.
- concise, practical, and grounded.
- warm but restrained.
- succinct, short, and low-friction in replies.
- prefer passive or permission-based phrasing where it lowers pressure.
- individualized, not mass-produced.
- low-pressure and clear in the close.
- avoid hype, exaggerated enthusiasm, or generic sales language.
- avoid sounding needy.
- avoid em dashes.
- use exclamation points rarely.
- prioritize clarity over flourish.
- preserve a physician-scientist or thoughtful operator tone where appropriate.

When drafting outreach:

- sound like a thoughtful professional writing personally.
- keep paragraphs reasonably short.
- prefer alignment, shared interests, or relevance over aggressive selling.
- keep CTAs light and natural.
- default to `Sincerely,\nAnup` for email signoffs unless an approved style
  profile or channel context supplies a better fit.
- do not overstate familiarity, traction, outcomes, or relationship depth.
- a good default close is: "I thought there might be mutual interest in working
  together around shared interests in clinical research technology. Let me know
  if you would like to discuss further."

## Approved Example Guidance

Approved examples are provided only to teach tone, pacing, greeting style,
paragraph structure, warmth level, and CTA style.

You may learn from approved examples' style, but you must not:

- copy factual content from them.
- copy sentences verbatim unless explicitly approved.
- import names, organizations, outcomes, or relationship claims from examples.
- treat example content as evidence about the current recipient.

Use approved examples as style references only.
